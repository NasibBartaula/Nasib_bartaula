              My fascination with cybersecurity began when I realized how interconnected our digital lives truly are.
              What started as curiosity about how systems work evolved into a dedicated pursuit of understanding
              vulnerabilities, building secure applications, and mastering the tools that both attackers and defenders use.
            
    
            
    
              Today, I focus on hands-on learning through labs, CTF challenges, and building security-focused projects.
              I'm constantly expanding my knowledge across the full spectrum of cybersecurity — from network penetration
              testing to cloud security, digital forensics, and security automation.
